About

Analytics & BI Leader · Data Visualization SME · 9+ years across Jackson National Life, Red Hat, and Infosys.

I'm a BI and analytics leader with 9+ years running enterprise reporting environments at Fortune 500 scale — Jackson National Life, Red Hat, and Infosys. I own the work most reporting programs avoid: rationalizing tangled dashboard estates, standing up governance that doesn't slow people down, modernizing pipelines so production stays production, and building the operating rhythms — intake, QA, Community of Practice — that turn analytics from a service desk into a function.

My current role at Jackson is hands-on across the full stack: Tableau platform governance as site administrator for two enterprise sites, Azure Data Factory pipelines, Power BI dashboards for C-suite and VP audiences, and coaching for the BI analysts building alongside me. I'm also the SME others bring in when a reporting program is stuck — on the architecture, the stakeholder framing, or the build. My leadership has been through SME ownership, coaching, and co-leadership — driving governance, enablement, and executive reporting across functions through influence and craft rather than reporting line.

I'm a Dean's Fellow and Momentum Scholar in the MBA program at Kelley School of Business, Indiana University, focused on Strategy and Leadership. I'm also a published researcher: two peer-reviewed conference papers on data classification with 64+ citations, and a background in data visualization that goes back to academic research before it went to production systems.
